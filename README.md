KPN Sales & Operations Analytics 📊

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing Blinkit sales and operations data using Microsoft Excel to understand business performance across sales, customers, marketing, delivery operations, products, inventory, and customer experience.

The objective was to transform raw transactional data into meaningful KPIs, trends, and business insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

Note: This is a portfolio project created using a simulated dataset for learning and demonstration purposes. It does not represent Blinkit's actual internal business data.

🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis aims to answer key business questions such as:

How is overall sales and revenue performing?
Which products and categories contribute most to revenue?
How do different customer segments behave?
Which marketing channels and campaigns perform better?
What percentage of orders are delivered on time?
What are the major reasons for delivery delays?
How do customers rate their overall experience?
What does customer feedback and sentiment indicate?
Where can operational improvements be made?

💡 Key Insights

Some of the major findings from the analysis include:

The dataset generated ₹1.10 Cr+ in revenue across 5,000 orders.
Average Order Value was approximately ₹2,202.
Revenue remained relatively stable between 2023 and 2024.
69.4% of orders were delivered on time, while approximately 30.6% experienced some level of delay.
Customer ratings averaged approximately 3.34/5, indicating scope for improving customer experience.
Marketing campaigns generated approximately ₹3.22 Cr in revenue against ₹1.63 Cr+ in marketing spend.
Customer segmentation provides an opportunity to analyse and target different customer groups more effectively.

📌 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

Improve delivery efficiency by investigating major causes of delays.
Focus on customer experience by analysing negative feedback categories.
Optimise marketing campaigns based on ROAS and channel performance.
Develop targeted strategies for Premium, Regular, New and Inactive customers.
Identify high-performing products and categories to improve sales and inventory planning.
Monitor KPIs regularly through an interactive Excel dashboard.
