# Blinkit Sales & Operations Analytics 📊

## Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end **Blinkit Sales & Operations Analytics project built using Microsoft Excel**.

The objective is to analyse sales, customers, marketing, delivery operations, products, inventory, and customer feedback to identify meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Business Objectives

The project focuses on answering the following business questions:

- How is overall sales and revenue performing?
- Which products and categories contribute most to revenue?
- How do different customer segments behave?
- Which marketing campaigns and channels perform better?
- What percentage of orders are delivered on time?
- What are the major reasons for delivery delays?
- How do customers rate their overall experience?
- What does customer feedback and sentiment indicate?
- Where can operational improvements be made?

---

## Dataset

The project contains multiple datasets covering different business areas.

### Main Data Sources

**Product Master**

Contains product, category, brand, price, and margin information.

**Order Details**

Contains order-level transaction, revenue, payment, and delivery information.

**Item Details**

Contains products, quantities, and unit prices associated with orders.

**Customers**

Contains customer segments and purchasing behaviour.

**Customer Feedback**

Contains customer ratings, sentiment, and feedback categories.

**Marketing Campaign**

Contains campaign spend, revenue, channels, and ROAS information.

**Delivery Performance**

Contains delivery time, distance, delivery status, and delay reasons.

**Inventory**

Contains stock received and damaged stock information.

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Sales Analysis 📈

Sales performance was analysed using:

- Total revenue
- Order volume
- Quantity sold
- Average Order Value
- Year-wise revenue
- Product performance
- Category performance

This analysis helps understand revenue trends and identify important sales contributors.

---

### 2. Customer Analysis 👥

Customers were divided into four segments:

- New
- Regular
- Premium
- Inactive

The analysis focuses on:

- Customer distribution
- Order behaviour
- Customer value
- Purchasing patterns
- Segment-level performance

This helps identify opportunities for targeted customer strategies.

---

### 3. Marketing Analysis 📣

Marketing performance was analysed using:

- Campaign spend
- Revenue generated
- Marketing channels
- Campaign performance
- ROAS

The objective was to understand how efficiently marketing investments generated revenue.

---

### 4. Delivery Analysis 🚚

Delivery operations were analysed using:

- Delivery time
- Delivery distance
- Delivery status
- Delay reasons

#### Delivery Performance

- **69.40%** of orders were delivered on time.
- **20.74%** were slightly delayed.
- **9.86%** were significantly delayed.

This highlights delivery efficiency as an important area for operational improvement.

---

### 5. Customer Experience Analysis ⭐

Customer feedback was analysed using:

- Customer ratings
- Sentiment
- Feedback categories

Feedback categories include:

- Delivery
- Customer Service
- Product Quality
- App Experience

The overall average customer rating was approximately **3.34 out of 5**.

---

### 6. Product & Category Analysis 📦

Product and category-level analysis was performed to understand:

- Revenue contribution
- Quantity sold
- Product demand
- Category performance

This helps identify high-performing products and categories.

---

## Key Insights 💡

The analysis generated several important observations:

- The dataset generated more than **₹1.10 Cr in revenue** across 5,000 orders.
- The average order value was approximately **₹2,202**.
- Revenue remained relatively stable between 2023 and 2024.
- **69.4% of orders were delivered on time**, while approximately 30.6% experienced some level of delay.
- The average customer rating was approximately **3.34/5**.
- Marketing campaigns generated approximately **₹3.22 Cr in revenue** against ₹1.63 Cr+ in marketing spend.
- Customer segmentation provides opportunities to develop targeted strategies for different customer groups.

## Business Recommendations 💼

### Improve Delivery Efficiency

Investigate major delay reasons and identify operational bottlenecks to improve on-time delivery.

### Improve Customer Experience

Analyse negative customer feedback to identify recurring issues related to delivery, customer service, product quality, and app experience.

### Optimise Marketing

Compare campaign and channel-level ROAS to identify opportunities for more efficient marketing spending.

### Target Customer Segments

Develop different strategies for New, Regular, Premium, and Inactive customers.

### Improve Product Planning

Identify high-performing products and categories to support better sales and inventory planning.

### Monitor Business KPIs

Use an interactive Excel dashboard to regularly monitor important business metrics.
